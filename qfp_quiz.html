공식 한 장 (펼치기)
  1. Full (dirty) spot price: S0 = B0 + AI0, AI = (days since last coupon / days in coupon period) × coupon
  2. Adjusted futures price (Schweser "F0"): F0 = FV(B0 + AI0) − AIT − FVCI. FV uses (1 + r)^(T) with T = days / 360
  3. Quoted futures price: QFP = F0 / CF = [FV(B0 + AI0) − AIT − FVCI] / CF
  4. AIT is accrued interest at futures expiration: (days since last coupon + days to expiry) / coupon period × coupon
  5. Mispricing: market QFP > equilibrium → futures overvalued → sell futures, buy bond. Profit at T = CF × QFP(market) + AIT − FV(B0 + AI0 − PVCI), then discount to today.
